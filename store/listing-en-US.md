## Full description (max 4,000 characters)

```text
JIIT Timer is a clean, offline interval timer for HIIT workouts. Set your prepare, work, rest, rounds and cool down times, save the program, and start training.

No account. No ads. No tracking. Everything stays on your device.

FEATURES

• Custom workout programs
Create, rename and reuse programs with your own work, rest, rounds, prepare and cool down times.

• Accurate interval timer
A drift-free timer based on elapsed real time keeps your workout on schedule, even if the app is busy or the screen is off.

• Pause, resume and skip
Pause at any time, resume exactly where you stopped, or skip to the next interval.

• Works in the background
A persistent notification keeps the timer running while you use other apps or turn off the screen, with Pause, Resume, Skip and Stop controls.

• Sound and vibration cues
Clear audio cues for work, rest and the 3-2-1 countdown, with optional vibration. You can turn either one off in Settings.

• Built for the gym
A large countdown, current round, next interval and total remaining time are visible at a glance, in light or dark mode.

• English and Spanish
The interface is available in both languages.

• Private by design
JIIT Timer does not use the internet, does not require an account and does not collect personal data.

JIIT Timer is an interval timer, not a medical device. Listen to your body and train within your limits.
```
