JIIT Timer Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

JIIT Timer is an offline HIIT interval timer for Android published by Javier Garcia Alonso ("the developer", "we"). This page explains what the app does with your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information. The app has no accounts, no network access, no advertising SDKs and no analytics libraries.

Data stored on your device

Workout programs and app settings (sound and vibration) are stored locally on your device using Android DataStore. They are used only to provide the app's functionality and are not sent to the developer or to any third party.

If you have Android system backup enabled, Android may include the app's local data in your device backup. That backup is controlled by your device and your Google account, not by JIIT Timer.

Permissions

  • Notifications: used to show the timer and its controls while a workout is running.
  • Foreground service: used to keep the timer running when the app is in the background or the screen is off.
  • Vibration: used for optional workout cues.
  • Wake lock: used to keep the timer running with the screen off.

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or network permissions.

Children

JIIT Timer is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children because the app does not collect personal information at all.

Changes

If a future version of the app starts collecting data or using third-party services, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
